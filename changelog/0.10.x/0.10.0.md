# 0.10.0 — 2026-10-14

## Added

- **`azure-devops` vendor** ([#56](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/56)) — reads the Azure DevOps health API and status RSS feed; one component per service and geography, no maintenance windows. New `api_type` value `azure-devops`; the registry grows to 53 vendors.
- **`component_scope_prefix`** on registry entries and the `devops-status://vendors/{name}` resource — an entry that shares a status page with other products reports only its own components, and the incidents and maintenance windows touching them.
- **`devops_check_dns`** reads `CAA` and `SOA` records ([#70](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/70)).
- **`devops_get_incidents`** takes `until` (latest start date) and `match` (words in the title or affected component names) ([#71](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/71)), and `full_updates` to read every update of an incident that alone exceeds the response budget.
- **`group`** on `devops_status_check` / `devops_watch_stack` components ([#62](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/62)) — the product or region the status page files a component under. `component_filter` matches group names too, and degraded components read `Group / Leaf` in `affected_components` and `degraded_components`.
- **`deferred_vendors`**, **`deferred_domains`**, **`deferred_resolvers`** ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/69)) — entries past a per-call cap come back verbatim for resending (20 vendors, 10 domains, 5 distinct resolvers), with a `notice`.
- **`notice`** on `devops_list_vendors` for an empty result ([#73](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/73)), in `structuredContent` and `content[]`.
- **`persistence_notice`** and error reasons **`vendors_required`** and **`stack_too_large`** on `devops_watch_stack`.
- **Non-HTTP TLS ports** (IMAPS, SMTPS, DNS over TLS) on `devops_check_certs`: the certificate is graded and HSTS reads "not checked" ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/65)).

## Changed

- **Breaking — `devops_watch_stack` over HTTP with `MCP_AUTH_MODE=none`** saves no stacks ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/66)); a call without `vendors` fails `vendors_required`. Stdio and `jwt`/`oauth` deployments save stacks as before.
- **Breaking — `devops_watch_stack`** with more than 20 `vendors` on a deployment that saves stacks fails `stack_too_large`; `vendors` lists over 100 (and `resolvers` over 20) are rejected.
- **Breaking — `DEVOPS_STATUS_CERT_TIMEOUT_MS`** must be an integer 1000–15000 and **`DEVOPS_STATUS_DNS_TIMEOUT_MS`** 1000–10000, or the server fails at startup through the configuration banner ([#60](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/60)).
- **Breaking — `sendgrid`** reads Twilio's status page and reports only SendGrid's components, incidents, and maintenances ([#54](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/54)).
- **Over-cap outputs are served at the cap** ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/69)): `limit` over 50, `component_limit` over 500, `timeout_ms` over 15000 (certs) or 10000 (DNS) are clamped, and `notice` says so.
- **Vendor update bodies** are plain text on every backend ([#63](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/63)); vendor-authored text in `content[]` is escaped and quoted as data.
- **`devops_check_dns`** queries resolvers over the DNS wire protocol (UDP, TCP on truncation), so TXT records are no longer split, NODATA and NXDOMAIN are read from the response code, and `timeout_ms` is one deadline per domain and resolver ([#64](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/64)).
- **`domains` entries** on `devops_check_dns` and `devops_check_certs` accept URLs, `host:port`, and Unicode names; each is reduced to one host ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/68)). An unusable entry is its own `error` row and never fails the batch.
- **`devops_check_certs`** sends SNI, retries once with TLS 1.0/1.1 enabled so legacy-only servers are inspected and graded `critical`, and bounds the connection, handshake, retry, and HSTS read by one `timeout_ms` ([#65](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/65)).
- **`devops_get_incidents`** holds each response surface to 64,000 bytes, ending a page between incidents and disclosing the next `offset` ([#58](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/58)); empty-result guidance names `since`, `until`, and `match` misses.
- **`devops_suggest_action`** for a vendor outside the registry suggests `devops_list_vendors` first, not a `devops_get_incidents` call that would fail ([#72](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/72)).
- **`devops_list_vendors`** `query` ignores surrounding whitespace.
- **AWS** component names carry the region code; **Status.io** multi-region components become a group with one leaf per region.
- Bun 1.4.2 → 1.4.3 (`packageManager`, Dockerfile base images); synced `framework-skills/`.

## Removed

- **Breaking — `invalid_domain`** error reason on `devops_check_dns` and `devops_check_certs`; a protocol prefix is stripped, and an unusable entry is reported in its own result row.

## Fixed

- **`devops_check_dns` record values** are escaped in `content[]` ([#75](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/75)); `structuredContent` keeps them as resolved.

## Security

- Saved stacks are isolated per authenticated caller ([#66](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/66)).
- The status-fetch cache holds at most 32 MiB serialized, and an upstream response body is read to at most 8 MiB ([#67](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/67)).
- `devops_check_dns` queries at most 5 distinct resolvers per call ([#69](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/69)).
- A `domains` entry is length-bounded before it is parsed ([#68](https://github.com/cyanheads/devops-status-mcp-server/issues/68)).
